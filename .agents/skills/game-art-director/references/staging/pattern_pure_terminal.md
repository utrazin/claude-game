# Staging Pattern: Pure Terminal / OS (CRT Displays & Document Desk)

> **Agent Directive:** Activate this staging pattern when `GDD.md` specifies an in-universe computer monitor, analog terminal, security console, or bureaucratic inspection desk (e.g., Papers Please, Her Story, Duskers, Hypnospace Outlaw, Uplink).

---

## 1. Visual Hierarchy & Budget of Attention

The game frame simulates **physical analog hardware or an inspector's workstation**:
* **50% - Primary Display Glass:** Central CRT monitor or terminal showing data logs, video feeds, or CLI buffers.
* **35% - Document Workbench:** Physical papers, passports, diskettes, and inspectable evidence laid out on the desk.
* **15% - Hardware Bezel & Controls:** Physical monitor bezel, power rocker switches, status LEDs, and ventilation grills.

---

## 2. Mandatory Contract Workbooks for `design.md`

### Section A: Display Glass & CRT Surface Contract
* Optical properties of the terminal screen:
  * Subtle barrel curvature at corners emulating cathode ray tube glass.
  * Scanlines ($1\text{px}$ alternating luminance falloff).
  * Phosphor persistence (subtle ghosting trail behind moving text/vectors).
  * Monospace dot-matrix or stencil typography.

### Section B: Physical Documents & Workbench Tools Contract
* All manipulable paperwork must be specified as physical objects:
  * Official documents with frayed edges, fold creases, and imperfect ink stamp coverage.
  * ID photos attached via metal staples or yellowed adhesive tape.
  * Physical inspection stamps with dual states: raised (in hand) and stamped (ink deposit on page).

### Section C: Hardware Chassis Controls Contract
* Controls must reflect period-accurate analog hardware (1970s–1990s industrial electronics):
  * Chunky rotary knobs for brightness/contrast.
  * Mechanical toggle switches with physical lever displacement.
  * **Strict Prohibition:** Never generate modern web form controls or flat mobile buttons.
