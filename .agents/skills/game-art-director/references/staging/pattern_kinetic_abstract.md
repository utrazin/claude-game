# Staging Pattern: Kinetic Abstract (Pure Geometry & Flow)

> **Agent Directive:** Activate this staging pattern when `GDD.md` specifies non-figurative geometric play, rhythmic dodging, vector combat, or pure kinetic reflexes (e.g., Geometry Wars, Super Hexagon, Tetris Effect, Thumper, Pong).

---

## 1. Visual Hierarchy & Budget of Attention

There are **zero figurative characters, narrative props, or painted environments**:
* **80% - Kinetic Flow & Geometry:** Player vector shape, incoming threat polygons, and high-energy particle bursts.
* **20% - Integrated Typographic Scale:** High-contrast score and combo numerals rendered directly into the background plane.

---

## 2. Mandatory Contract Workbooks for `design.md`

### Section A: Geometric Actor Contracts
* Strict mathematical geometric specifications:
  * Controllable Vector: Dominant apex vertex pointing toward the movement or aiming vector.
  * Hostile Polygons: Regular polygons (triangles, diamonds, octagons) with procedural rotation around center of mass.
  * Ammunition / Beams: Single-pixel or high-luminance lines with linear decay trails.

### Section B: Particle & Shockwave Flow Contract (Core Juice)
* Because surfaces lack static texture, visual weight relies on the particle pipeline:
  * Directional fragmentation: Shards inherit collision velocity vector.
  * Expanding circular shockwaves: Unfilled single-stroke rings with linear expansion and alpha decay.
  * Trail/Ghosting: Previous position echoes calibrated by speed.

### Section C: Integrated Typographic Plane (Replaces Traditional HUD)
* Score and multiplier meters rendered in clean, sans-serif or monospace type anchored to screen center-back or bottom edge.
* **Strict Prohibition:** Traditional health bars and modal game-over dialog boxes are forbidden. Game over triggers kinetic freezing or typographic zoom with instant zero-click restart.
