# Style Baseline: Chalk & Slate

> **Agent Directive:** Use this reference as a modular aesthetic baseline for analog acoustic minimalism, limestone chalk on natural slate, drafting diagram aesthetics, and dry textural purity (e.g., Minit, Baba is You, Gato Roboto, World of Goo - Sketch).

---

## 1. Materiality & Surface Treatment

* **Primary Materials:** Matte slate stone, crumbly limestone chalk, dry compressed charcoal, felt eraser trails.
* **Contour & Edges:** Dry, textured stroke boundaries with microscopic grain imperfections simulating chalk dragged across rough stone.
* **Surface Finish:** Pure matte dryness. Erased areas leave subtle chalk-dust ghosts providing faint spatial depth.
* **Lighting Model:** Completely diffuse ambient light. Zero specular glints, reflections, or chromatic blooms.

---

## 3. Calibration Instructions for `game-art-director`
1. Use line density and stroke thickness rather than color gradients to communicate entity mass.
2. Ensure interactive elements use opaque chalk marks while background grids use faint dust strokes.
3. Completely ban all blur and bloom shaders.
